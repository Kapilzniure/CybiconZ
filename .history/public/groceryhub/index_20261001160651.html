1.0 Project Background & Objectives

Executive Summary

Grocery Hub has achieved significant visibility in the Rockdale market following recent successful social media campaigns (e.g., Prajita). The primary objective of this engagement is to deploy a highly structured, professional content strategy to convert this initial attention into consistent, recurring foot traffic and increased average order values.

"Views do not pay the rent. Our goal is to transition Grocery Hub from unpredictable viral spikes to a reliable, weekly sales engine."

Strategic Objectives

  • Sustain Market Visibility: Establish a high-quality weekly content schedule to ensure Grocery Hub remains top-of-mind.
  • Drive High-Margin Sales: Shift promotional focus toward bulk items (e.g., 20kg rice) and premium goods (e.g., fresh goat meat).
  • Ecosystem Integration: Implement cross-promotion strategies with the salon to maximize customer lifetime value within the specific Grocery Hub ecosystem.

1.0 Project Background & Objectives

Executive Summary

Grocery Hub has achieved significant visibility in the Rockdale market following recent successful social media campaigns. The objective of this engagement is to deploy a structured, professional content strategy to convert this initial attention into consistent, recurring foot traffic and increased average order values.

Strategic Objectives

  • Sustain Market Visibility: Transition from viral, unpredictable spikes in attention to a consistent, high-quality weekly content schedule.
  • Drive High-Margin Sales: Shift promotional focus toward bulk items (e.g., 20kg rice) and premium goods (e.g., fresh goat meat).
  • Ecosystem Integration: Implement cross-promotion strategies with the salon to maximize customer lifetime value within the Grocery Hub ecosystem.
2.0 Scope of Work

Agency Responsibilities

CybiconZ Digital operates as an external offshore creative department. Our team will handle the ideation, design, and strategy, allowing the Grocery Hub Board to focus purely on operations.

Core Deliverables

  • Weekly Shoot Directives: We provide structured scripts and shot lists. The client's only operational requirement is recording raw footage on a mobile device.
  • Premium Graphic Design: High-conversion digital posters for combo deals, new arrivals, and community announcements.
  • Short-Form Video Editing: Professional editing of raw footage into high-retention formats (Reels/TikToks) optimized for the algorithm.
  • Strategic Direction: Monthly alignment on featured products, sales targets, and community events.
